About StudentHub Initiative

StudentHub was initiated by the Faculty of Technology and Engineering (FTE) at Charotar University of Science and Technology (CHARUSAT). Our goal is to streamline communication, automate event registrations, and simplify campus management for students and faculty.

Our Core Objectives

Centralized Communication

No more searching across bulletin boards or chat groups. All notices, timetables, and notifications are centralized.

Easy Event Management

Students can browse tech, cultural, and sports events with detailed itineraries and register with a single click.

Student Accessibility

Built with web standards, semantic HTML5, and accessible UI principles to ensure seamless usability across devices.

College Accreditation

CHARUSAT is accredited with an 'A+' grade by NAAC and recognized among premier educational institutes in Gujarat.
