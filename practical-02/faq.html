Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Find answers to common questions about StudentHub, registration, and event participation.

General Queries

Q1: Who can register on StudentHub?

Answer: Any enrolled student of CHARUSAT FTE across undergraduate (B.Tech, BCA) and postgraduate (MCA, M.Tech) programs can register using their student email ID.

Q2: Is there any registration fee for campus events?

Answer: Most university-organized workshops and cultural activities are completely free. Certain specialized hackathons or national conferences may carry a nominal fee mentioned on the event details page.

Q3: How do I know if my registration is confirmed?

Answer: Once you submit an event registration, you will see it listed under your personal Student Dashboard with a "Confirmed" badge.

Q4: What should I do if I forgot my login password?

Answer: Please reach out to the campus computer center or submit a request via our Contact Page.
